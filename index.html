About

Operations-first, customer-focused

I build my work around one idea: an e-commerce order isn't finished until the customer is genuinely satisfied — even, and especially, when something has to be returned. Over the past several years I've focused on B2C e-commerce operations: returns and refunds, inventory accuracy, courier coordination, and the day-to-day discipline that keeps a distribution operation honest.

At BRAC-Aarong, I supervise a team responsible for return orders and backend inventory processing — reviewing eligibility, issuing RMAs, inspecting returned stock, and flagging fraudulent orders that would otherwise erode margins. Earlier, as Outlet E-commerce Manager at Shwapno (ACI Logistics Ltd.) and Outlet Manager at Khaas Food Ltd., I managed full outlet operations — inventory, staffing, budgets, promotions and compliance — for physical retail alongside e-commerce fulfillment.

I hold a B.B.A. Honours in Management from Habibullah Bahar University College, and I bring a practical, data-informed approach to sales, customer management and problem solving — grounded in day-to-day retail and logistics realities rather than theory alone.
